# Pilot users — onboarding

Pilots are voluntary, transparent and small. Only the maintainer recruits and talks to pilot users.

1. **Agree on scope in writing (e-mail is fine):** which workflow they try, for how long,
   what they share. Make clear Lunima is open-source research software, not a product with
   support guarantees.
2. **Confidential designs stay with the user.** Never upload a participant's design to an
   external service or to this repository. If they want help on a design, work on their
   machine or on a sanitised example they create.
3. **Setup:** latest release from https://github.com/aignermax/Lunima/releases, the
   relevant example, a 30-minute walkthrough call.
4. **Observe, don't steer:** let them try the workflow on their own first; note where they
   stop (`FEEDBACK.md`).
5. **Record:** an anonymised summary as `discovery/interviews/I-NNNN.md` (consent noted).
   Behaviour (they used it on their own design, came back, asked for more) is E3 evidence.
