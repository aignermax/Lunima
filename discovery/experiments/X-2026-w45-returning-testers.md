# X-2026-w45-returning-testers: Did people who already tried Lunima come back and use it on their own work?
- status: proposed
- method: interview
- autonomy: needs-approval
- assumption: People other than the maintainer who tried Lunima have since used it again on their own work, without being asked to.
- hypotheses: H0, H1, H4
- budget: 0 USD. Maintainer time: at most 3 conversations of 30 min, plus 15 min write-up each. No new contacts, only people who have already used Lunima.

## Why this, why now (context, not part of the pre-registration)
H0's riskiest assumption is "current users stay and grow without new workflows". Nobody has
checked whether those users exist. The repository holds only second-hand mentions of outside use,
with no first-hand summary.

People who already know Lunima are the cheapest source of a first E2 entry: no cold outreach, no
landing page, no new feature. Their answers also show *which* workflow brought them back (visual
editing → H1, circuit simulation → H4, something else) or what they used instead. That gives a
real comparison of workflows, which the Q1 outcome asks for.

Warm contacts are biased towards kindness, so colleagues and friends are reported separately and
weighted down.

## Pre-registration
- who counts: anyone other than the maintainer and the agents who used Lunima at least once
  before 2026-11-02. That includes workshop participants, people who filed issues or sent
  feedback, and colleagues who tried it.
  - Before the first conversation, the maintainer lists these people privately, in the order they
    first used Lunima, and contacts them in that order. No picking the keenest.
  - Colleagues and friends are marked *warm*.
- success: ≥ 2 of the first 3 people interviewed describe at least one session after their first
  contact in which they used Lunima on their own design, study or research task. The session was
  not at the maintainer's request, and they name the task and roughly when it happened. At most one
  of those two may be a warm contact.
- failure: either of these:
  - ≤ 1 of the first 3 describes such a session.
  - The maintainer's private list has fewer than 3 people by 2026-11-20. Record the count: a list
    that short means the user base H0 relies on is not there yet.
- does not count: praise, intentions ("I would use it if…"), downloads, stars, or sessions the
  maintainer set up or ran.
- measurement: the maintainer runs the conversations with the interview guide `returning-testers` (past-behaviour questions only, no demo or pitch beforehand) and counts, per person, whether a qualifying unprompted session on their own task is described. Anonymised summaries, with the participant's consent, go to `discovery/interviews/I-NNNN.md`, and one dated line per interview goes here under `## Results`.
- timebox: 2026-12-04
